Accessibility

Built in

  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark themes. Muted text is tuned for both.
  • Visible focus rings on every interactive element (:focus-visible).
  • Touch targets of at least 44px for buttons, nav links, inputs and switches.
  • A skip link (.mn-skip) on every template.
  • Tabs follow the ARIA pattern: arrow keys, Home and End.
  • The modal uses native <dialog>: Escape closes it and focus returns to the opener.
  • The mobile sidebar closes with Escape. The theme button exposes aria-pressed.
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns off animations, including the ticker.
  • No text smaller than 12px.

Your part

  • Give icon-only buttons an aria-label.
  • Label every input with <label for>. Point errors at the field with aria-describedby and add aria-invalid="true".
  • Give charts an aria-label with the actual numbers.
  • Don’t rely on color alone: status badges also carry words.
  • Check contrast again if you change brand colors.
